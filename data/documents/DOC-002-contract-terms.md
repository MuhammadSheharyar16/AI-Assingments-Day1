## 1. Contract Structure

Every supplier agreement is made up of three parts: the signed order form, the standard terms and conditions, and any schedules attached at the point of signature.

Where the parts conflict, the order form takes precedence over the schedules, and the schedules take precedence over the standard terms. A supplier's own terms of sale have no effect and are expressly excluded, whether or not they are referenced on a quotation, acknowledgement or invoice.

Contract references follow the format CTR-YYYY-NNNN. Live agreements referenced in this document include CTR-2024-0117, CTR-2025-0042 and CTR-2025-0088.

## 2. Term and Commencement

The initial term begins on the commencement date stated in the order form. Where no commencement date is stated, the term begins on the date of the last signature.

The standard initial term is twenty four months. A shorter or longer initial term may be agreed where the sourcing category approval records the reason.

Work performed before the commencement date is performed at the supplier's own risk and creates no payment obligation, even where a purchase order has been raised.

## 3. Renewal and Extension

An agreement may be extended beyond its initial term only by a written extension signed by both parties. There is no automatic renewal and no rollover by conduct.

The total of all extensions may not exceed the length of the initial term. An agreement with a twenty four month initial term may therefore run for no more than forty eight months in total.

An extension does not reset the notice provisions and does not reopen the commercial terms. A change to price, scope or service level must be handled through change control, not through the extension itself.

Where an extension is not agreed before the expiry date, the agreement ends on the expiry date. Continued supply after that point does not create a new agreement and is not payable.

## 4. Notice Periods

Either party may end an agreement for convenience by giving sixty days written notice to the other party. Notice runs from the date of receipt, not the date of issue.

This notice period supersedes any notice period stated in the sourcing policy or in any earlier version of these terms. Where an older document states a longer period, the sixty day period in this section applies.

Notice must be given in writing to the contract manager named in the order form, with a copy to the Commercial and Legal mailbox. Notice given verbally, or given only to an operational contact, has no effect.

A notice once given may not be withdrawn except with the written agreement of the other party.

## 5. Termination for Convenience

Termination for convenience takes effect at the end of the notice period. The supplier is paid for goods delivered and services performed up to the effective date, and for committed costs the supplier can evidence and could not reasonably avoid.

The supplier is not entitled to loss of profit, loss of opportunity or any sum in respect of the unexpired part of the term.

During the notice period the supplier must continue to perform in full. Reduced performance during a notice period is itself a breach.

## 6. Termination for Cause

An agreement may be cancelled with immediate effect where the supplier commits a material breach that is not capable of remedy, or fails to remedy a breach within thirty days of a written remedy notice.

Immediate termination is also available where the supplier becomes insolvent, enters administration, has a receiver appointed, ceases to trade, fails a sanctions screen, or is found to have provided false information during onboarding or evaluation.

Termination for cause does not require the sixty day notice period and does not entitle the supplier to payment for committed costs.

The exercise of a right to terminate for cause does not prevent the organisation from recovering losses arising from the breach.

## 7. Assignment and Subcontracting

The supplier may not assign or novate the agreement, in whole or in part, without the prior written consent of the organisation. Consent may be withheld without giving reasons.

A change of control of the supplier is treated as an assignment for the purposes of this section and must be notified within ten working days of completion.

Subcontracting of any part of the supply requires prior written approval. The request must name the subcontractor, describe the scope proposed, and confirm that the subcontractor has passed an equivalent compliance screen. Approval of a subcontractor does not transfer any obligation away from the supplier, which remains fully responsible for performance.

The organisation may withdraw approval of a subcontractor where that subcontractor fails a compliance screen or fails to perform.

## 8. Change Control

Any change to scope, price, specification, service level or delivery profile must be recorded in a written change note signed by both parties before the change takes effect.

A change note states the change requested, the reason, the commercial effect, the effect on the term, and the date from which the change applies. An unsigned change note has no effect.

Work performed in anticipation of an unsigned change note is not payable. Verbal instruction from an operational contact does not constitute a change note.

## 9. Dispute Resolution

Disputes are escalated in three stages. The contract managers of both parties attempt resolution within fifteen working days. If unresolved, the dispute is escalated to a commercial director on each side for a further fifteen working days. If still unresolved, the parties may proceed to mediation before any litigation is commenced.

Performance continues throughout a dispute. Suspension of supply during a dispute is a material breach unless the dispute concerns an accepted health and safety risk.

## 10. Restricted Content

The signatory authority matrix, the internal commercial approval thresholds and the named legal reviewer for each agreement are restricted content. They are available to Commercial and Legal and to the Procurement Governance Board only.

Signed agreements are retained for seven years from the end of the term.
