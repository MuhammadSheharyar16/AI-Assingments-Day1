# DOC-001 — Supplier Selection and Sourcing Policy

**Document ID:** DOC-001
**Version:** 2.0
**Owner:** Procurement Governance, Meridian Procurement Ltd
**Classification:** Internal — Synthetic Training Material

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy sets out how Meridian Procurement Ltd identifies, evaluates and appoints suppliers for goods and services. It applies to every sourcing activity carried out on behalf of the organisation, including direct awards, competitive events and framework call-offs.

The policy covers the point at which a requirement is raised through to the point at which a supplier is added to the approved supplier list. It does not cover the commercial terms of the resulting agreement, which are governed separately.

Where this policy refers to a supplier, the same obligations apply to a vendor appointed under any framework, sub-agreement or intermediary arrangement.

## 2. Sourcing Categories

Requirements are classified into one of three sourcing categories before any market activity begins. The category determines the level of scrutiny applied and the approval route required.

Category A covers requirements with an annual value below fifty thousand pounds. A single written quotation is sufficient, provided the requester records why the chosen supplier represents value.

Category B covers requirements with an annual value between fifty thousand and five hundred thousand pounds. A minimum of three written quotations must be obtained and a scored evaluation must be recorded.

Category C covers requirements with an annual value above five hundred thousand pounds. A formal sourcing event is mandatory, the evaluation panel must contain at least three members, and the award recommendation must be approved by the Procurement Governance Board.

Category boundaries are assessed on total contract value over the full term, not on the value of the first year alone. Splitting a requirement into smaller awards to avoid a category threshold is prohibited.

## 3. Evaluation Criteria and Weighting

All Category B and Category C events are scored against a fixed criteria set. The weightings below apply unless a documented exception is approved before the event opens.

Price and total cost carries a weighting of thirty five percent. Technical capability and specification compliance carries a weighting of thirty percent. Delivery performance and capacity carries a weighting of twenty percent. Compliance, sustainability and social value carries a weighting of fifteen percent.

Each criterion is scored on a scale of zero to five by every panel member independently. Scores are moderated in a single recorded session. A supplier scoring zero against any mandatory compliance criterion is excluded from further evaluation regardless of the total score achieved.

The weighted score is calculated to two decimal places. Where two suppliers finish within one point of each other, the panel must record a written rationale for the preferred award.

## 4. Approved Supplier List

A supplier may only receive a purchase order once it appears on the approved supplier list. Entry to the list requires a completed onboarding record, a passed compliance screen and a signed agreement.

The current approved supplier list for component and logistics categories includes Nordvale Components Ltd, Brackenfield Logistics Ltd, Halvorsen Industrial Supply and Petronella Services Ltd.

Each entry on the list records the categories the supplier is approved for, the date of approval, the date of next review and the name of the responsible category manager. A supplier approved for one category may not be issued orders in another category without a fresh evaluation.

Entries are reviewed at least every twenty four months. A review may be brought forward at any time by the category manager or by Procurement Governance.

## 5. Single-Source Justification

A single-source award is an award made without competition where competition would normally be required. Single-source awards are permitted only in defined circumstances and always require written justification.

Permitted grounds are: a genuine sole supplier in the market; an urgent operational need arising from an event outside the organisation's control; continuity of an existing installation where a change of supplier would create disproportionate technical risk; or the exercise of an option contained in an existing agreement.

Cost saving alone is not a permitted ground. Preference based on a prior working relationship is not a permitted ground.

The justification must name the ground relied on, describe the alternatives considered, state the value and duration of the proposed award and be approved at one level above the normal approval route for that category.

## 6. Sourcing Event Timelines

Category B events allow a minimum response period of fourteen calendar days from issue of the invitation. Category C events allow a minimum response period of twenty eight calendar days.

Clarification questions are accepted up to five working days before the response deadline. Answers are issued to all participants at the same time and without attribution to the questioner.

A response received after the stated deadline is not evaluated. The only exception is a documented failure of the organisation's own submission system, which must be confirmed by the system owner.

## 7. Re-evaluation and Removal from the List

A supplier is re-evaluated when its performance score falls below the agreed threshold for two consecutive review periods, when a compliance screen is failed, when ownership or control of the business changes, or when the category itself is re-competed.

Where the outcome of a re-evaluation is removal, the supplier is entitled to ninety days written notice of withdrawal from the approved supplier list. During that period existing purchase orders continue to run but no new orders may be raised.

Removal on the grounds of a failed compliance screen, insolvency, or a sanctions match takes effect immediately and the notice period stated above does not apply.

A removed supplier may reapply after twelve months. Reapplication requires a full onboarding record and a fresh evaluation; the previous approval carries no weight.

## 8. Records and Restricted Content

Every sourcing event produces a record containing the requirement, the category assessment, the participant list, the individual and moderated scores, the award recommendation and the approval trail.

Individual panel member scores, the named contacts of participating suppliers, and the comparative scoring matrix are restricted content. They are visible to Procurement Governance and to the category manager only. They are not disclosed to participants, to other suppliers or to requesters.

Records are retained for seven years from the date of award or from the date the event was abandoned.
