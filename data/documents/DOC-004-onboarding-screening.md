# DOC-004 — Supplier Onboarding, Compliance and Screening

**Document ID:** DOC-004
**Version:** 1.1
**Owner:** Supplier Assurance, Meridian Procurement Ltd
**Classification:** Internal — Synthetic Training Material

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This document describes the checks a supplier must pass before it can be added to the approved supplier list, and the checks that are repeated during the life of the relationship.

Onboarding is a prerequisite for approval. A supplier that has completed evaluation but not onboarding may not be issued a purchase order.

## 2. Onboarding Stages

Onboarding runs in four stages and each stage must be closed before the next begins.

Stage one is registration. The supplier submits its legal name, registration number, registered address, trading addresses and primary contact details.

Stage two is documentation. The supplier provides the documents listed in section three.

Stage three is screening. Supplier Assurance runs the financial, sanctions and compliance checks described in sections five to seven.

Stage four is activation. The supplier record is created in the finance system, bank details are verified, and the approval record is written to the approved supplier list.

A supplier that fails to complete onboarding within ninety days of registration has its application closed and must restart.

## 3. Documentation Requirements

The following documents are mandatory at stage two: certificate of incorporation, most recent filed accounts, evidence of insurance cover, a signed anti-bribery declaration, a modern slavery statement where the supplier meets the turnover threshold, and a completed data protection questionnaire where the supplier will hold or process personal data.

Documents must be current at the date of submission. A document that expires during screening must be replaced before activation.

Where a supplier is part of a group, documents are required for the contracting entity, not for the parent, unless the parent is providing a guarantee.

## 4. Financial Standing Check

Supplier Assurance obtains an independent financial standing report for every supplier at onboarding and at each subsequent review.

A supplier scoring below the minimum acceptable band may still be approved where a mitigation is put in place. Acceptable mitigations are a parent company guarantee, a performance bond, a reduced payment term, or a limit on the total exposure permitted at any one time.

The mitigation applied is recorded against the approval and is reviewed at the same frequency as the standing report.

A material adverse change in financial standing is a re-evaluation trigger and must be raised to the category manager within five working days of it being identified.

## 5. Insurance Requirements

Every supplier must hold and maintain public liability cover of at least five million pounds for the duration of the agreement.

Where the supplier provides professional advice or design work, professional indemnity cover of at least two million pounds is also required. Where the supplier transports goods on the organisation's behalf, goods in transit cover appropriate to the maximum consignment value is required.

Certificates are collected at onboarding and at each renewal date. A lapse in cover suspends the supplier from receiving new orders until cover is restored and evidenced.

## 6. Sanctions and Export Control Screening

Every supplier, every director of that supplier, and every approved subcontractor is screened against the applicable sanctions lists at onboarding.

Screening is repeated on a rolling basis and on the occurrence of any re-screening trigger. A positive match suspends all activity with that supplier immediately and is escalated to Supplier Assurance and Commercial and Legal on the same working day.

Where goods or technical data are subject to export control, the supplier must confirm the classification and hold any licence required. The organisation does not accept a supplier's assurance in place of the classification itself.

A supplier may not be released from suspension by an operational contact. Release requires a written decision from Supplier Assurance.

## 7. Modern Slavery, ESG and Data Protection

Suppliers above the statutory turnover threshold must publish a modern slavery statement and provide the link at onboarding. Suppliers below the threshold complete a short declaration instead.

The organisation reserves the right to audit a supplier's labour practices on reasonable notice. Refusal to permit an audit is a re-screening trigger.

Where a supplier processes personal data on the organisation's behalf, a data processing agreement is required before activation. The supplier must list its sub-processors, notify any addition in advance, and report a personal data breach without undue delay and in any event within seventy two hours of becoming aware of it.

## 8. Bank Verification and Activation

Bank verification must be completed before the first payment is released. Verification is carried out by an independent callback to a telephone number obtained from the supplier's registration record, never from a number supplied on an invoice or in email correspondence.

Any subsequent change to bank details triggers the same verification process and suspends payment until verification is complete.

A request to change bank details received by email alone is treated as a suspected fraud attempt and is reported to Finance Operations before any action is taken.

## 9. Re-screening Triggers

Screening is repeated when any of the following occurs: a change of ownership or control, a change of registered address or contracting entity, a lapse or change in insurance, a material adverse change in financial standing, a refused audit, a sanctions list update affecting the supplier's jurisdiction, or the approach of the twenty four month review date.

The category manager and Supplier Assurance are jointly responsible for raising a trigger. A trigger raised by either party is sufficient to start re-screening.

## 10. Restricted Content

Bank verification records, the callback log, individual financial standing scores, named compliance contacts and the details of any active fraud investigation are restricted content.

They are accessible to Supplier Assurance and Finance Operations only. They are not disclosed to the supplier, to the category manager, or to any requester.

Onboarding records are retained for seven years from the date the supplier is removed from the approved supplier list.
